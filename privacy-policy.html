Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-28

Purpose

ltth.app uses TikTok Login Kit only to authenticate a user who chooses Sign in with TikTok and to retrieve the basic account identity needed to associate that login with the current ltth.app session.

Data we do not collect through Login Kit

ltth.app does not use TikTok Login Kit to collect unrelated TikTok data. It does not use Display, Content Posting, or other TikTok APIs or scopes through this sign-in feature.

Retention and deletion

Login-related data is retained only as needed for the associated ltth.app session and local account configuration. To request access to, deletion of, or information about Login Kit data, contact rainer@dominik.in.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning TikTok Login Kit, email rainer@dominik.in.
