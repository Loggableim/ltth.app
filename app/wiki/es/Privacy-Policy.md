# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-07-04
**Product:** PupCid's Little TikTool Helper (`ltth.app`)

This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles data in normal operation.

## 1) Data We Process
The app primarily runs on your machine and stores data locally in your profile directories.

Typical local data:
- Stream profile settings
- Plugin settings
- Event logs and analytics data generated by your stream session
- Optional backup and export files

## 2) Optional Cloud Sync
If you enable cloud sync, selected config data is written to a cloud folder that you configure yourself (for example a folder in OneDrive/Drive/Dropbox or another local sync directory).

In this mode:
- we sync files from your own path,
- we do not run a managed remote database service for your private stream data.

## 3) Third-Party Services
Some features use external providers (for example TikTok-related adapters or TTS engines).

Each external service is governed by its own privacy policy.

You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to use those services with your account.

## 4) Cookies and Tracking
The software itself does not run web tracking for ad or analytics business purposes.

Most dashboard interactions happen locally through standard browser sessions used by the app.

## 5) Data Retention
Data is kept as long as your local or sync storage remains configured.

You can delete:
- local profile folders,
- logs,
- backups,
- plugin data,

to remove stored information.
