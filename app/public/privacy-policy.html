Privacy Policy

ltth.app is primarily a local streaming helper with data processed on your machine.

1) Data We Collect

  • Stream-related settings and local config files.
  • Plugin settings and event logs generated during your usage.
  • Optional logs, backups and sync files.

2) Optional Cloud Sync

If enabled, user-controlled folder sync is used (for example OneDrive/Google Drive/Dropbox-style folders). We sync only the files your chosen path contains. Your sync provider is configured by you.

3) Third-Party Services

  • Some integrations depend on TikTok-related endpoints and plugin providers.
  • Credentials/session material is handled only as required by each integration.
  • External services may process data under their own privacy policies.

4) Cookies and Analytics

No ad-tracking framework is bundled for website-style analytics in normal use.

5) Your Rights

You can remove local data by deleting local profiles, logs, and backups from your app directories. If you enabled sync, remove synced copies from your sync location as needed.
